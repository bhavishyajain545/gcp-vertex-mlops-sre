Why this is SRE, not just MLthe same ideas as a service release: an objective gate instead of a reviewer's gut feeling, a canary with traffic split instead of a swap, SLOs on what users see, a feedback signal (drift) with an automated response, and every identity keyless with least privilege. The pipeline is the release process; the registry is the audit log.
